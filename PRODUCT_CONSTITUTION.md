# Uchit OS Product Constitution

## Mission
Uchit OS must continuously converge toward the smallest, clearest, fastest correct implementation of the approved Uchit product, methodology and business workflow.

## Optimization order
1. Correctness
2. Clarity
3. Minimality
4. Speed
5. Reliability
6. Security
7. Automation
8. New features

## Product laws
- One canonical source of truth per business fact.
- Simplify before adding.
- Every operational surface should make the next action obvious.
- Preserve Client/Case/Project/Floor context rather than asking repeatedly.
- Locked methodology remains deterministic; AI may assist but may not invent truth.
- Business gates are server-authoritative.
- Significant history is versioned/audited; do not silently overwrite protected records.
- Protected uncertainty fails closed.
- Working architecture is preserved unless evidence justifies change.
- Idle is a valid state; agents do not invent work to stay busy.
- Autonomy exists to reduce friction, not create activity.

## UX North Star
Uchit OS should feel smaller than the complexity underneath it: current state -> what matters -> one clear next action.
